## Creative Guidelines

### Image Recommendations:
1. Professional headshot with results overlay
2. Before/after career transformation
3. Laptop with income dashboard
4. Fortune 100 company logos (with disclaimer)
5. Student testimonial screenshots

### Video Recommendations:
1. Hook in first 3 seconds (pattern interrupt)
2. Problem agitation (30 seconds)
3. Solution introduction (30 seconds)
4. Social proof (15 seconds)
5. Offer + CTA (15 seconds)

### Targeting Suggestions:
- Age: 28-45
- Interests: Career development, Professional growth, Leadership, AI, Side hustles
- Behaviors: Engaged shoppers, Online course purchasers
- Lookalike: 1% of existing customers

### Budget Allocation:
- 40% to Ad Set 1 (Curiosity Gap)
- 30% to Ad Set 3 (Godfather Offer)
- 20% to Ad Set 4 (AI Income)
- 10% to Ad Set 2 (Hook-Story-Offer)
